Avoiding Scams and Unethical Practices

The real estate market can be rife with scams, especially for first-time buyers. Here are essential tips to safeguard yourself:


    Always verify the legal documents of the property.
    Request a site visit and do not rely solely on pictures.
    Never make payments in cash; always opt for bank transfers for traceability.
    Check for the completion certificate and occupancy permit.
    Consult a real estate lawyer for guidance on contracts.
    Stay clear of deals that seem too good to be true.

Understanding the Documentation Process

One common mistake is not fully understanding the documentation process involved in buying real estate. It is essential to be aware of:


    The Sale Deed: A legal document proving the sale of property.
    The Title Deed: Proof of ownership and transfer of property.
    The Encumbrance Certificate: Validates that the property is free from legal dues.
    The No Objection Certificate (NOC): Essential for legal clearance.


Being informed about these documents can prevent potential legal hassles in the future.
